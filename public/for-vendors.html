How listings work

Our initial US directory uses public information from official business websites. Listings show a business name, category, city and a link to the official site. Source information and review dates are kept with the directory records.

What couples see

Couples can filter by category and city, browse the map and open your website. They contact you directly for pricing, appointments and availability. We do not create ratings or take bookings on your behalf.

A growing directory

Current categories include bridal boutiques, venues, photographers, florists and caterers. Self-service listing submissions are not available yet.
