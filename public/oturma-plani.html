Seating Planner

Bring your seating plan to life

Choose your tables, add your guests and drag them into seats near the stage, along the aisle or wherever suits them best.

Setup

Guest list

  • Enter one guest per line. Add an optional group and preference: Full Name | Group | stage/aisle.
  • Combine table groups, such as 6 tables of 6, 5 tables of 4 and 7 tables of 5.
  • Auto seat your guests first, then fine-tune the plan with drag and drop.
  • Add pairs of names to help seat guests together or keep them at separate tables.

Seating rules

  • Enter two names per line: Emma Wilson | James Wilson
  • The planner tries to seat pairs at the same table and keep conflicting guests at separate tables.

Venue objects

Add an object, then drag it into position in the main room or additional area.
